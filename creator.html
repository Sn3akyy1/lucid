How it got here

From a bar and a dock to a whole desktop.

Lucid started as a bar and a dock on Quickshell. Every release since has taken over another part of the desktop: widgets on the wallpaper, a settings app that runs the machine, and finally the lock screen, the password prompt and the login screen, so nothing looks borrowed.

It is built in the open. People open issues and pull requests on the public repo, and their work ships in the next release with their name on it.

The mark

Lucida, the brightest star.

The logo is named for the astronomical term for the brightest star in a constellation, which shares its Latin root, lux, with Lucid. A broken orbit ring, a four-point star at the centre, and one companion dot outside the gap.

Its official colour is coral. In the shell, the ring and the dot follow your accent, so the mark on the dock's launcher button recolours with your palette. Pick a palette on this site and the mark in the bar does the same.
