How it got here

From a bar and a dock to an ecosystem.

Lucid started as a bar and a dock on Quickshell. Every release since has taken over another part of the desktop, until the files, the pictures and the pointer were its own too. It is built in the open: contributors' work ships in the next release with their name on it.

The mark

Lucida, the brightest star.

The astronomical name for the brightest star in a constellation shares its Latin root, lux, with Lucid: a broken orbit ring, a four-point star and one companion outside the gap. Its colour is cyan, #00F0FF. In the shell the ring and the companion follow your accent, and here they follow the palette you pick.
